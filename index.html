Magnetic interference testbed

3 Axis Gimbal Magnetic interference testbed

  • The problem: Magnetorquer interference can affect magnetometer readings used for satellite attitude determination.
  • What I built: A three-axis gimbal with two magnetometers, ESP32 wireless streaming, and a switchable solenoid—so rejection approaches can be investigated on a laptop.

The approach

Reproduce the disturbance. Test the response.
  • Use a switchable solenoid to introduce a magnetic disturbance while rotating the gimbal.
  • Stream IMU data over Wi-Fi to observe how motion and interference affect the readings.
  • Develop and compare candidate noise-rejection methods on a laptop, with the goal of protecting attitude estimates from magnetorquer interference.
